## About me

I am an AI Engineer focused on building and benchmarking production-oriented LLM systems. My current interests sit at the intersection of **LLM inference**, **fine-tuning**, **RAG**, **AI agents**, and **GPU performance engineering**.

- Building tenant-aware AI assistants and tool-using agents
- Benchmarking throughput, latency, VRAM usage, caching, and context efficiency
- Turning research ideas into reproducible, observable, deployable systems
- Open to collaborating on practical AI infrastructure and developer tools
